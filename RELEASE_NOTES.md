## EasyBoard 0.3.0

The first public release of EasyBoard: a soundboard for Windows, macOS and Linux that plays your
sounds through your microphone in Discord, OBS, games and any other app.

### Soundboard
- Sound tiles with names, pictures, colors and IDs. Sort, search and filter them by color.
- Global hotkeys for every sound, plus hotkeys for looping all sounds and muting.
- Loop single sounds or all of them at once.
- Separate volumes for what you hear and what others hear, and your own microphone mixed in.
- Runs in the tray, and can be controlled from the command line.

### Sound editor
- Record, open, drop or paste audio, then cut, copy, paste, crop and add silence.
- Effects: speed, pitch, volume, fades, reverse and a compressor, on the whole sound or just a part.
- Undo and redo. Save as WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, Opus or M4A.

### Sound library
- Search and preview sounds from myinstants.com and download them for your board.

### App routing
- Send the sound of other apps (music, videos, games) through your microphone while you keep hearing it.

### Themes
- Built in: *Liquid Glass*, *Kitty*, *Starry Night* and *OLED*.
- Make your own, or add one from a .zip file. See `THEMES.md` on GitHub for how.
- Or use a picture of your own as the background, with blur, opacity and brightness.

### What you need
- **Windows 10 or 11** and VB-Cable (free, from vb-audio.com/Cable). App routing needs Windows 10 version 2004 or newer.
- **macOS 12 or newer** and VB-Cable. App routing needs macOS 14.2 or newer.
- **Linux** with PulseAudio or PipeWire. Nothing else to install.
